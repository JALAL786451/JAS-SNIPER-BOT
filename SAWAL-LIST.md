# Fishing Lots — Mukammal Sawal List

Yeh sawal us tarteeb se hain jis tarteeb se aap asal mein trade karte hain.
Shuru mein saada, phir aage barhte hue. Isi liye upar se neeche jawab dein,
kyunke har sawal ka jawab agle sawal ko samajhne mein madad karta hai.

**Kaise jawab dein**
- Sirf number likh kar jawab de dein, misaal `12. Haan, hamesha 0.01`
- Jo sawal aap par laagu na ho, us par likh dein `laagu nahi`
- Jis ka jawab "pata nahi" ya "kabhi socha nahi" hai, woh bhi likh dein.
  Yeh bhi ek jawab hai aur is se yeh pata chalta hai ke kahan andaza lagana
  parega
- Aakhir mein Section L khali chhora hai. Wahan aap apne sawal khud bana kar
  khud jawab likh dein, jo cheezein maine poochi hi nahi

---

## Section A — Account aur auzaar

1. Aap ka account cent hai ya standard? Balance kitna hai abhi?

2. Leverage kitna hai? Kya aap ne kabhi badla?

3. Swap bilkul sifar hai? Kya yeh hamesha rehta hai ya kabhi lagta bhi hai?

4. Trade aap Exness ki app se karte hain ya MT5 se? Dono se?

5. MA 20 aur 50 simple hain ya exponential? Kis qeemat par lagi hain, close par?

6. Chart par in do lakeeron ke ilawa aur kya laga hua hai?

7. Aap ka SMC Coach Pro v2 wala script isi chart par chalta hai ya woh alag
   TradingView par hai?

---

## Section B — Fishing, yani aam haalat

8. Pehli lot lagane ke liye MA 20 aur 50 ka kya haal hona chahiye? Cross
   hona zaroori hai, ya faasla, ya rukh?

9. Cross ke foran baad lagate hain ya thora intezar karte hain?

10. Kya aap candle band hone ka intezar karte hain, ya kisi bhi waqt laga
    dete hain?

11. Lot hamesha 0.01 hi hoti hai?

12. Munafe par band karne ka theek number kya hai? Aap ne "1 dollar ya fees
    se kuch upar" kaha tha. USC mein woh kitna banta hai?

13. Kya yeh target hamesha ek jaisa rehta hai, ya market tez ho to badal
    dete hain?

14. Aam haalat mein ek waqt par ek hi lot khuli hoti hai, ya kai?

15. Agar lot lagate hi munafa aa jaye to foran band kar dete hain ya thora
    aur dekhte hain?

16. Ek din mein aam taur par kitni baar fishing hoti hai?

17. Koi waqt aisa hai jab aap trade nahi karte? News, koi khaas session,
    market khulne ka waqt?

---

## Section C — Jab musibat shuru hoti hai

18. Aap ne "teen ya chaar lots" kaha tha. Woh sab ek hi taraf hoti hain?

19. Woh teen chaar lots kis wajah se lagayin? Har ek ki apni alag wajah
    thi, ya ek hi soch par lagati chali gayin?

20. Reversal ka pehla ehsaas aap ko kaise hota hai? Chart par sab se pehle
    kya cheez nazar aati hai?

21. Jo teen dollar ka nuqsan aap ne bataya, woh ek lot par hota hai ya sab
    lots milaa kar?

22. Us teen dollar wale nuqte par aap sab se pehle kya dekhte hain?

23. Chaar dollar tak pohanch kar bhi faisla na ho to phir kya karte hain?

---

## Section D — Reversal parhna

24. Aap ke chart ke bayein taraf jo panel hai, us mein theek kya kya nazar
    aata hai? Agar mumkin ho to us ki tasveer bhej dein.

25. Kaunse timeframes us mein hain? 1M, 5M, 15M, 30M, 1H, 4H, D, W?

26. LG aur FVG, in dono mein se koi ek zyada ahem hai ya dono barabar?

27. Kaunsa timeframe aap ko sab se zyada sahi lagta hai?

28. Kya ek hi timeframe ka signal kaafi hai, ya do teen ka ek saath milna
    zaroori hai?

29. Agar chhota timeframe ek baat kahe aur bara timeframe uski ulti, to aap
    kis ki maante hain?

30. Reversal "pakka" kab hota hai? Woh kaunsi cheez hai jise dekh kar aap
    kehte hain ke ab yeh waqai mur gaya?

31. Jhoota reversal kaisa dikhta hai? Kabhi dhoka hua? Us waqt kya hua tha?

---

## Section E — Rukh badalna

32. "Ab munasib nahi" ka faisla aap kaise karte hain? Koi number hai, ya
    tajurbe ka ehsaas?

33. Rukh badalne ke baad pehli nayi lot kab lagate hain? Foran ya intezar?

34. Recovery lot aur against lot mein theek farq kya hai? Dono ka maqsad
    alag alag lafzon mein bata dein.

35. Kya aap ek waqt mein dono lagate hain, ya pehle ek phir doosri?

36. Aap ne "unstability" ka lafz istemal kiya tha. Chart par woh kaisi
    nazar aati hai? Kis cheez ko dekh kar aap ko lagta hai ke ab unstable
    hai?

---

## Section F — Barabar karna aur book jama dena

37. Misaal ke taur par abhi 5 sell aur 2 buy khuli hain. Aap theek kya
    karenge? Kitni lots aur kis taraf?

38. Kya aap bilkul barabar karte hain, ya ek taraf thori zyada rakhte hain?

39. Barabar karne ka faisla kis nuqte par hota hai? Kya koi nuqsan ka
    number hai, ya lots ki ginti, ya equity?

40. Equity ka koi khaas number hai jahan aap kehte hain ke bas, ab aur
    nahi?

41. Kya kabhi aisa hua ke barabar karne se pehle hi equity khatam hone
    lagi? Us waqt kya kiya?

42. Jab book jam jati hai, us waqt kul nuqsan aam taur par kitna hota hai?

43. Jamane ke baad kya kabhi aur lots lagate hain, ya bilkul haath rok lete
    hain?

---

## Section G — Intezar

44. Aam taur par kitni dair intezar karna parta hai? Minute, ghante, ya
    din?

45. Sab se lamba intezar kitna raha hai jo aap ko yaad ho?

46. Sote waqt koi alarm ya alert lagate hain, ya bas so jate hain?

47. Kya kabhi jami hui book chhor kar poora din bahar rahe hain?

---

## Section H — Hedge kholna

48. Reversal aane par sab se pehle kya band karte hain?

49. "Hedge wali lots" se aap ki murad kaunsi lots hain? Jo aakhir mein
    barabar karne ke liye lagayi thin, ya jo us waqt munafe mein hain?

50. Jo 0.03 se 0.05 ka nuqsan aap ne bataya, woh har lot par hota hai ya
    sab milaa kar?

51. Kya saari hedge lots ek saath band karte hain, ya thori thori kar ke?

52. Hedge band karne ke baad jo taraf bachti hai, us ka target kya hota
    hai? Kahan tak chalne dete hain?

53. Agar hedge kholne ke baad pata chale ke reversal jhoota tha, to phir
    kya karte hain?

---

## Section I — Close All

54. Close All kab dabate hain? Zero se upar aate hi, ya koi muqarrar number
    hota hai?

55. Kya kabhi nuqsan mein bhi Close All kiya hai? Kis surat mein?

56. Close All ke baad foran nayi fishing shuru karte hain ya thora rukte
    hain?

---

## Section J — Paisa, lots aur hadd

57. Lots ki ginti ka koi hisaab hai jo aap dimagh mein rakhte hain? Misaal,
    itni lots se zyada nahi.

58. Kitni lots khulne ke baad aap ko fikr hone lagti hai?

59. Kya aap ne kabhi 0.01 se bari lot lagayi? Kyun aur kab?

60. Din ka koi target hai, ya koi had jahan aap us din ke liye ruk jate
    hain?

---

## Section K — Bure din, aur yeh sab se ahem section hai

61. Sab se zyada kitni lots ek saath khuli hain aap ki? Andaza bhi chalega.

62. Book sab se bura kitna neeche gayi hai? Yani jama shuda nuqsan sab se
    zyada kitna dekha?

63. Kya kabhi account khatam hone ke qareeb pohancha? Kya hua tha?

64. Kya kabhi aisa hua ke tareeqa bilkul kaam nahi kiya? Us din kya alag
    tha?

65. Aap ke apne khayal mein yeh tareeqa kis surat mein nakaam ho sakta hai?
    Kis cheez se aap ko sab se zyada khatra lagta hai?

---

## Section L — Jo maine poocha hi nahi

Yahan aap apne sawal khud banayein aur khud jawab likh dein.

Jo cheezein aap karte hain aur upar kisi sawal mein nahi aayin. Koi chhoti
aadat, koi qaida jo aap ne khud banaya, koi cheez jo aap ne tajurbe se
seekhi. Jo bhi zehen mein aaye.

Yeh section shayad sab se qeemti niklega, kyunke jo mujhe maloom nahi woh
main poochh bhi nahi sakta.

---

## Aakhir mein

Jaldi ki koi zaroorat nahi. Aaram se, thora thora kar ke likhein. Adhoora
jawab bhi bhej sakte hain, main us par kaam shuru kar dunga aur baqi baad
mein le lunga.
