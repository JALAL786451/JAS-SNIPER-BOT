Bunyadi lafz

Ye samajh lein to baqi sab aasan hai

O · H · L · C

O = candle khulne ki qeemat. H = sab se oonchi. L = sab se neechi. C = abhi ki qeemat (band hone par close).

TF (timeframe)

Ek candle kitne waqt ki hai. 15m = har candle 15 minute. 1D = poora din. Gold ka din OANDA par ab ~03:00 PKT shuru hota hai (1 Nov ke baad ~04:00).

Pip

Is script mein gold ka 1 pip = $0.10. Yani $1 ki harkat = 10 pip. Cent account par 0.01 lot se 1 pip = 0.1 USC.

R (khatre ki ikai)

Entry se SL tak ka fasla. Har nateeja isi mein gina jata hai: −1R = poora SL, +2R = khatre ka do guna faida.

Entry 4130, SL 4127 → 1R = $3. 0.01 lot par $3 (USD demo) ya 3 USC (cent). TP1 (1R) = 4133, TP2 (2R) = 4136.

Exp (expectancy)

Band trades ka ausat nateeja, R mein. Musbat = ab tak ausat faida, manfi = nuqsan, 0 ke qareeb = barabar. Spread is mein shamil nahi.

SELL +0.25R (52) = 52 trades, kul taqreeban +13R.

Swing, BOS, CHOCH

Swing high = woh candle jis ke 3 daen aur 3 baen candles us se neeche hon (3 candle baad pakka hota hai). Candle swing ke paar band ho to toot. Rukh ke saath toot = BOS. Rukh ke khilaf toot (rukh palat gaya) = CHOCH.

BOS/CHOCH chart par likhe nahi aate, magar zones aur ishare inhi se bante hain.

1 · Baen table

Har TF ki chalti candle, ek row mein

1m par 9 row (1m se 1W), 15m par 4 row (15m, 1H, 4H, 1D). Har row us TF ki abhi wali candle hai.

Rukh: ▲ UP · ▼ DN · = BARABAR

Abhi ki qeemat us candle ke open se upar (UP, hari), neeche (DN, laal), ya barabar.

1D ▲ UP: qeemat open 4112.73 se $19.98 upar. 3H ▼ DN: sirf $0.83 neeche.

Ye trend nahi, sirf adhoori candle ka rang. Asal rukh MA21/MA63 aur "1H rukh" batate hain.

O · H · L · C

Us TF ki chalti candle ke khulne, oonche, neeche aur abhi ke daam. C har row mein ek hi hota hai (chart ki abhi ki qeemat).

1W: O 4141.605, H 4184.385, L 4066.535 = is hafte ka $117.85 ka range.

Bhari

Body (|C − O|) range (H − L) ka kitna hissa hai. Zyada % = qeemat ek taraf gayi aur wahin rahi. Kam % = zyada wick, candle abhi faisla nahi kar payi.

3H 6%: $13.5 ghoom kar wapas open ke paas, taqreeban doji.

Upar ya neeche nahi batata. Woh Rukh mein hai.

FVG ↑ / ↓

Teen candles mein khali jagah (gap). ↑ = tez upar chaal ne neeche khala chhora (hara). ↓ = tez neeche chaal (laal).

3H ↑: abhi ki 3H ka low (4128.545) do candle pehle wali 3H ke high se upar hai.

Sirf aakhri 3 candles dekhta hai. Qeemat khale mein aayi to mit jata hai.

LGB ↑ / ↓ (liquidity grab)

↓ HARA hai: qeemat pichle swing low ke neeche gayi aur wapas upar band ho rahi hai. Neeche ke stop saaf ho gaye, ye bullish ishara hai. ↑ LAAL hai: swing high ke upar gayi aur wapas neeche aa gayi, ye bearish ishara hai.

Teer batata hai qeemat ne kahan jhaanka, trade ka rukh nahi. Candle band hone se pehle mit sakta hai.

Zone: BZ · SZ

BZ = candle ka low us TF ke pichle swing low tak pohncha. SZ = high pichle swing high tak pohncha.

Ye chart ke rangeen zone dabbe nahi hain. BZ ke saath LGB ↓ ho to matlab chhu kar wapas aaya (bullish). Sirf BZ ho to shayad abhi toot raha hai.

◄ aur row ka rang

Sirf FVG/LGB se banta hai. Hara = FVG ↑ ya LGB ↓. Laal = FVG ↓ ya LGB ↑. Narangi = dono. Baqi columns ka is par koi asar nahi.

3H hari (FVG ↑) halanke candle laal hai. 1W laal (FVG ↓).

Highlight hai, BUY/SELL ka hukm nahi.

2 · Daen table

Pressure, ishare ka haal, aur nateejon ka hisaab

SELL 55% (1m-1W) · Pressure

Har TF ki chalti candle mein abhi ki qeemat kahan hai: low par 0% BUY, high par 100% BUY. Formula: (C − L) ÷ (H − L) × 100. Phir sab TF ka seedha ausat liya jata hai (1m chart par 9 TF, 15m par 4). Ausat 50 se upar ho to "BUY x%", neeche ho to "SELL (100 − x)%".

1D (4132.705 − 4103.445) ÷ 39.98 = 73% BUY. 3H 31% BUY. Sab ka ausat 45% BUY tha, is liye table ne "SELL 55%" likha.

Ye kharidaron ka volume nahi aur peshangoi bhi nahi. 1m ki candle 1W jitni ginti hai, is liye number jaldi uchhalta hai. 1m aur 15m par alag % aana ghalti nahi, dono alag TF ginte hain.

Pressure ka rang aur ◄ 90%+ / ◄ 80%+

55% se zyada BUY = hara, 45% se kam BUY = laal, beech mein grey. Isi liye "SELL 55%" grey tha aur 15m ka "SELL 58%" laal. Ausat ishare ki hadd tak pohnche (1m par 90, 15m par 80) to ◄ aur rangeen background aata hai.

◄ ka matlab trade khulna nahi. Ye sirf pehli shart hai.

MTF 90%: intezar · 15m 80%: intezar | 1H rukh: DN

Ishare ki khayali trade ka haal. intezar = abhi koi trade khuli nahi. BUY khuli / SELL khuli = trade chal rahi hai. (TP1 ✓) = aadhi TP1 par band ho gayi aur SL entry par aa gaya.

1H rukh = pichli band 1H candle par MA21, MA63 se upar ho to UP, warna DN. 15m ka ishara sirf isi taraf banta hai.

1H rukh: DN tha, is liye us waqt sirf 15m SELL ban sakta tha.

KHULI: SELL 08 Oct 11:42 @ … SL … TP1 … TP2 …

Trade khulne par ye line poora plan likh deti hai. Waqt Pakistan ka hai aur us candle ke khulne ka hai. Entry us candle ka close hai (15m chart par 15 minute baad).

TP1 ke baad bhi is line mein purana SL likha rehta hai. Asal SL tab entry par hota hai, aur chart par "SL ab Entry par (BE)" likha aata hai.

Exp BUY - (0) | SELL +0.25R (52) · sirf 1m par

1m ke main ishare ka hisaab, yani 1m chart par BUY/SELL label wala ishara. Bracket mein band trades ki ginti. Nateeja: SL −1R, TP1 ke baad wapas entry 0R, TP3 +3R. Ulta BOS/CHOCH aaye ya 60 se zyada candle guzar jayen to jahan hai wahin band.

BUY (0): load hui history mein ek bhi BUY trade band nahi hui. Wajah ghaliban ye ke 4H aur 1D dono ka rukh neeche tha, aur filter tab BUY nahi hone deta.

Exp BUY -0.25R (28, jeet 36%) | SELL +0.01R (46, jeet 50%)

90% (1m) ya 80% (15m) ishare ka hisaab. Nateeja: SL −1R. TP1 ke baad wapas entry +0.5R. TP2 +1.5R. Waqt khatam (1m par 120 candle, 15m par 64 candle) to jahan hai. jeet = jo trades 0 se upar khatam huin.

1m BUY: 28 trades, 36% jeet, ausat −0.25R, yani kul taqreeban −7R. 15m: BUY 0R (31), SELL +0.07R (38).

Sirf jeet % se dhoka hota hai: +0.5R wali trade bhi "jeet" ginti hai. Aap ka 75% jeet wala sabaq yaad rakhein.

KAMZOR

Jab kisi taraf 10 ya zyada trades ho chuki hon aur Exp 0 ya kam ho, to agle ishare ka label grey ho kar "KAMZOR: is qaide ki Exp manfi" likhta hai.

Abhi 1m BUY (−0.25R) aur 15m BUY (0R, + ka nishan nahi) dono par agla ishara KAMZOR hoga.

Ye trade nahi rokta, sirf khabardar karta hai.

3 · Chart ki lakeerein

Chart par jo bana hai, us ka matlab

MA21 aur MA63

Neela-hara (cyan) MA21 aur gulabi (magenta) MA63, us chart ke apne TF ke close ki ausat lakeerein. Asal mein ye EMA hain, nayi candles ka wazan zyada. MA21 upar aur ADX 18 ya zyada = rukh UP. MA21 neeche aur ADX 18 ya zyada = rukh DN.

ADX chart par nahi dikhta. ADX 18 se kam ho to koi main ishara nahi banta, chahe lakeerein door hon.

Hare aur laal zone dabbe

BOS/CHOCH hone par pichle swing par zone banta hai. Upar toot ho to neeche swing low par hara (demand) zone. Neeche toot ho to upar swing high par laal (supply) zone. Zone ke saath ki lakeer swing ki asal qeemat (wick ki nok) par hoti hai.

Zone mit jata hai jab candle us ke paar band ho, qeemat 8 ATR door chali jaye, 300 candle purana ho, ya teesri dafa chhua jaye. Zyada se zyada 10 zone rehte hain.

Har TF ke apne zone hote hain. 1m ke zone chhote aur qareeb, 1D ke bare aur door.

FVG grey dabba aur LGB↑ / LGB↓ label

Sirf 1m chart par, sirf aakhri candle par. Matlab wahi jo baen table mein: LGB↓ hara = bullish, LGB↑ laal = bearish.

BUY / SELL label + Entry/SL/TP lakeerein (sirf 1m)

Main SMC ishara: zone ko chhoona, toot (breakout), ya MA21 par wapsi. Ye 4H/1D rukh, DXY, Supertrend, ADX, volume, chop aur score 60+ jaise kai filters se guzarta hai. Entry neeli, SL laal dashed, TP1/TP2/TP3 hari. Label par "LONG Score 72/100" aur "Risk 1% ≈ N units" likha hota hai.

Score ek checklist ki ginti hai. 80/100 ka matlab 80% jeetne ka chance nahi. "units" $1000 ke khayali account par hain, aap ke account ke lot nahi.

Ishare ka laal / hara dabba (1m par 90%, 15m par 80%)

Laal dabba = entry se SL (khatre ka ilaqa). Hara dabba = entry se TP2 (faide ka ilaqa). "TP1 (aadhi band)" par aadhi trade band hoti hai. Label "ABHI KHULI · 15m SELL 85%". Trade khatam hote hi sab mit jata hai.

Pivot · Resistance · Support

Kal ki daily candle ke H, L, C se: Pivot = (H + L + C) ÷ 3. Resistance = 2 × Pivot − L. Support = 2 × Pivot − H. Din bhar ek hi jagah rehti hain. Halki R2/S2 lakeerein par sirf qeemat likhi hoti hai.

Kal: H 4170.000, L 4066.535 → Pivot 4115.955, Resistance 4165.375, Support 4061.910.

Exness app ke number thore alag honge (din ka waqt alag). Setting "(khud)" mein Exness ke number likh sakte hain. In ka kisi ishare par asar nahi.

Chhupi cheezein: Supertrend aur naali

Dono default band hain. Supertrend chhupa ho kar bhi main ishare ko rokta hai (BUY ke liye Supertrend upar chahiye). Naali sirf dikhawa hai, kisi ishare par asar nahi.

Upar · Neeche

Candle ke andar saari upar wali chaalein jama (Upar) aur saari neeche wali (Neeche), pip mein.

Aaj Upar 5,048.3 pip ($504.83), Neeche 4,869.5 pip. Upar thora zyada, is liye din abhi tak upar hai.

Kul raasta

Upar + Neeche. Gaari ke meter jaisa: qeemat jitna bhi aayi-gayi, sab gina gaya.

Aaj 9,917.8 pip ($991.78) ka safar. Kal 33,769.7 pip ($3,376.97).

Ye kam se kam andaza hai. Asal tick-by-tick safar is se bhi lamba tha.

Asal farq ▲ ▼

Aakhri close − pehla open. Hamesha Upar − Neeche ke barabar.

Aaj +178.8 pip = +$17.88 (O 4112.73 se ~4130.6). Aaj ke open par 0.01 lot BUY hoti to cent account par taqreeban +17.88 USC (spread se pehle). Kal −553.1 = −$55.31.

Kaam %

Asal farq ÷ Kul raasta × 100. Gaari ki misaal: aap ne kitne kilometre chalaye (Kul raasta) aur ghar se aakhir kitne door pohnche (Asal farq). Kaam % batata hai chalne ka kitna hissa kaam aaya.

Aaj 178.8 ÷ 9,917.8 = 2%. Kal 553.1 ÷ 33,769.7 = 2%. Dono din barabar "aaye-gaye".

Kal gold $55 gira phir bhi 2% aaya. Poore din ko 1 minute se ginein to % hamesha chhota aata hai, is liye 2% ka matlab "koi trend nahi" nahi. Sirf barabar lambai ki candles milayein: din se din, poore ghante se poora ghanta. 1H abhi (7%, 23 minute) ko 1H pichla (3%, 60 minute) se mat milayein.

Range

H − L, pip mein. Candle ki poori oonchai.

Aaj 399.8 pip = $39.98 (4143.425 − 4103.445). Kal 1,034.7 pip = $103.47. Aaj ab tak kal ke range ka ~39% chala hai.

Minute

Kitni 1 minute candles gini gayin. Data ki jaanch ke liye.

Kal 1376 = taqreeban poora din (gold ka din ~1380 minute). Aaj 439 = ~7 ghante 20 minute.

Aam din par "kal" 1380 se bohot kam ho to data adhoora hai (ya chhutti ka chhota din tha).

POC (Point of Control)

Woh daam jahan qeemat ne sab se zyada waqt guzara. Har minute us minute ke low se high tak ke har pip par barabar baant diya jata hai, aur jis pip ke paas sab se zyada minute jama hon woh POC hai. Chart par narangi lakeer aur label "1D POC (aaj ki candle)".

Aaj POC 4109.550. Abhi qeemat ~4131 hai, yani POC se $21.5 upar, us jagah se door jahan aaj sab se zyada waqt guzra. Kal ka POC 4166.650 tha, aaj se ~$57 upar.

Bohot se traders POC ko us din ka "munasib daam" samajhte hain aur sochte hain ke beech ke bazaar mein qeemat us ki taraf wapas aati hai. Is script ne ye baat naapi nahi.

VA 70% (Value Area)

POC ke ird-gird woh patti jahan kam az kam 70% waqt guzra. Chart par do neeli dashed lakeerein, upar aur neeche.

Aaj 4103.445 – 4134.500: aaj ke 439 minute mein se kam az kam 307 minute is $31 ki patti mein guzre. Neeche wala sira din ka low hi hai.

Traders ise aise parhte hain:

  • VA ke andar: bazaar "barabar" hai, qeemat VA ke upar aur neeche wale kinaron ke beech ghoom sakti hai.
  • VA ke upar nikal kar wahan waqt guzare: naye daam "qubool" ho rahe hain (value upar ja rahi hai). Jaldi wapas andar aa jaye to "radd".
  • VA ke neeche: yahi baat ulti.

~4131 par qeemat aaj ki VA ke andar thi, upar wale kinare 4134.5 se ~$4 neeche. Agar 4134.5 ke upar nikle aur tike, to traders kahenge "aaj ki value se upar qubool".

Ye traders ka khayal hai, script ne test nahi kiya. VA khud bhi din bhar khiskti rehti hai.

Pattiyon ke rang

Narangi = POC wali patti. Neeli = VA ke andar (thora sa bhi). Grey = VA se bahar. Pattiyan aakhri candle se baen taraf banti hain, sirf aaj ki candles ke upar. 1m par (din ke pehle 10 minute ke baad) hamesha dikhti hain. 1D chart par aaj ki candle akeli hai, is liye wahan sirf lakeerein.

Tooltip (mouse rakhein)

Kisi khane par mouse rakhein to "Qeemat mein: …" yani dollar mein fasla dikhta hai. Range par H aur L bhi, Asal farq par O aur C bhi.

Ye qeemat ka fasla hai, account ka faida nahi. Cent account par 0.01 lot = $1 par 1 USC.

Trade ka idea

9 qadam, tarteeb se

Har qadam ke saath likha hai ke screen par kya dekhna hai. Ye sab pehle DEMO par.

  1. Khabar dekhein

    NFP, CPI, FOMC se 30 minute pehle se taqreeban 1 ghanta baad tak koi nayi trade nahi. Script mein khabar ka filter nahi, ye aap ko khud dekhna hai. NFP: 1 Nov ke baad 6:30 PM PKT.

  2. 15m chart kholein, data check karein

    PIP table ke upar v4.2 likha ho aur "1D abhi" ka Minute barh raha ho.

  3. Ijazat wali taraf likh lein

    Daen table: 1H rukh: UP = sirf BUY, DN = sirf SELL. Har ghante dobara dekhein, ye har 1H candle ke band hone par badal sakta hai.

  4. Pressure dekhein

    Ishare ke liye ausat apni taraf 80%+ ho aur chaaron TF (15m, 1H, 4H, 1D) usi taraf hon.

    8 Oct ki misaal: 1D candle 68% BUY par thi. Jab tak qeemat 1D ke beech (4123.435) se upar thi, 15m SELL ban hi nahi sakta tha.

  5. Din ke level dekhein

    POC, VA ki lakeerein, pivot aur zone: kya TP1 se pehle koi level raaste mein hai? Script sirf zones dekhta hai. POC, VA aur pivot aap ko khud dekhne hain.

  6. Bazaar ka mizaaj dekhein

    PIP table ka Kaam % aur Range. Sirf maloomat ke liye: kisi Kaam % ki hadd ko filter ke taur par test nahi kiya gaya.

  7. Ishare ka intezar karein

    Khud pehle mat kudein. Daen table 15m 80%: SELL khuli (ya BUY) likhe aur chart par dabba bane, tab. Screen ghoorne ke bajaye alert lagayein: Create Alert → SMC Coach Pro v4 → "MTF 90% SELL" ya "MTF 90% BUY", Once Per Bar Close. 15m chart par yehi naam 15m ishare ke liye kaam karte hain.

  8. Exp aur KAMZOR dekhein

    Aakhri line mein us taraf ka Exp dekhein. KAMZOR ho to bhi demo par lein aur alag likhein, taake ginti poori rahe.

  9. Demo par, script jaisa, phir likhein

    Do alag 0.01 lot (har ek 0.01 hi). TP1 par ek band karein aur doosri ka SL entry par le aayein. TP2 par doosri band. SL lage to band. 64 candle (16 ghante bazaar) mein na SL na TP2 ho to band. Har trade likhein: waqt, taraf, %, entry, SL, TP1, TP2, kaise band hui, kitne R, kitna spread.

    OANDA aur Exness ke daam thore alag hain, is liye plan dollar ke fasle se copy karein. Lot kabhi na barhayein, nuqsan ke baad dugna na karein.

Shor ka andaza: 1R ÷ √(trades). Nateeja tabhi kuch kehta hai jab shor ka taqreeban do guna ho, aur woh bhi spread ke baad. Ye number spread ke baghair hain aur sirf chart par load hui history ke hain.

15m ishare ko tab sabit maanein jab:

  • DEMO par aage se 100+ trades, bilkul inhi qaidon se.
  • Spread ke baad ausat kam az kam +0.2R.
