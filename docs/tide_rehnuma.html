Teen baatein

Indicator trade nahi karta

Ye sirf batata hai kab, kis qeemat par, kitna lot aur SL kahan. Order aap khud MT5 mein lagate hain.

Sirf BAND candle

Faisla din ki candle band hone ke baad hota hai. Chalti candle ka level chhoo lena kaafi nahi.

Haar zyada, jeet bari

Ye trend ka system hai. Aksar chhota nuqsan, kabhi kabhi bohot bari jeet. Bari jeet ko kaatna mana hai.

Qaide

Chaar qaide, isi tarteeb se

Panel ki pehli chaar rows yahi chaar qaide hain.

  1. Rukh: D1 ka EMA50 aur EMA200

    EMA50 upar ho to sirf BUY. Neeche ho to sirf SELL. Chart ka halka hara ya laal rang yahi batata hai.

  2. Bazaar zinda ho

    ATR(14) qeemat ka kam az kam 0.25% ho. Bohot susti mein breakout jhoote hote hain.

  3. Naali tooti

    Din ki candle pichhli 10 candles ke sab se oonche high ke upar band ho (BUY). SELL ke liye 10 candles ke sab se neeche low ke neeche. Chart ki hari aur laal seedhi lakeerein yahi do level hain.

  4. SL aur us ka sarakna

    Shuru ka SL entry se 2 × ATR door. Phir har din SL pichhli 5 candles ke sab se neeche low tak aage aata hai (SELL mein sab se oonche high tak). SL kabhi peeche nahi jata.

Trade band hoti hai: SL lagne par, sarakte SL par, ya D1 ka rukh palatne par. Koi TP nahi. 8 Oct ke Tester mein 459 mein se 420 trades sarakte SL par, 38 shuru wale SL par, aur 1 rukh palatne par band hui.

Lot1% risk kitne paise hai, aur lot ka formulaSettings mein apna balance likhein (neeche dekhein).
R kya hai

1R = shuru ka SL faasla (2 ATR). +3R ka matlab jitna khatra liya tha us ka teen guna kamaya. −1R = poora SL laga.

Lot ka formula

lot = risk ÷ (SL ka faasla $ × 100). Cent account par 1.00 lot = $1 harkat par 100 USC. Standard par 1.00 lot = $1 harkat par $100. Dono par yahi formula chalta hai.

Lot 0.01 se kam nikle to trade na karein. Balance kam hai aur SL bara.

Apni settings (ek dafa)

Panel ke naam par ⚙ → Inputs → 4 · Lot ka hisaab: Balance (account ki currency mein), Currency (USC ya USD), Risk 1%. Demo USD account par 3 · Kharcha aur swap mein "Swap BUY" 0.51 likhein. Live cent account swap-free hai, wahan 0 rakhein.

Roz ka kaam

Har subah, 5 minute

  1. Chart kholein

    OANDA:XAUUSD, 1D. Panel ke upar "chart 1D (yahi naapa gaya)" aur "2R aadhi: BAND" likha ho.

  2. Khuli trade hai?

    Haan: "SL / 2R" row ka SL MT5 mein laga dein. "Aakhri baat" mein "D1 rukh palta" ya "band" likha ho to MT5 mein bhi trade band kar dein.

  3. Naya signal aaya?

    Chart par kal ki candle par BUY/SELL teer, aur "Aakhri baat" mein "BUY khuli" ya "SELL khuli". To MT5 mein order lagayein (agla hissa).

  4. Kuch nahi?

    Chart band kar dein. Zyada tar din yahi hoga. Hafton koi signal nahi aata, aur ye bilkul theek hai.

MT5

Order lagana aur SL sarkana

  1. New Order

    XAUUSD par New Order kholein. Type: Market Execution. BUY signal par Buy, SELL par Sell.

  2. Volume

    Panel ki "Der se entry?" row wala lot. Ye abhi ki qeemat aur wahi SL se bana hai.

  3. Stop Loss

    Panel ka SL bilkul wahi likhein. Take Profit khali chhor dein.

  4. Har subah SL badlein

    Positions mein trade par double-click → Modify → Stop Loss panel wala → Modify. SL sirf faide ki taraf jata hai.

    MT5 naya SL qubool na kare kyunke qeemat pehle hi us ke paar hai, to trade usi waqt band kar dein. Tester bhi yahi karta hai.

Ek account, ek kaam. Aap ki Fishing Lots tokri wale account par JasDeskView aur JasJoriClose har lot ginte hain. Woh Tide ki trade ko bhi jori mein daal sakte hain. Tide ki trade alag account par chalayein: abhi demo, baad mein alag live account.

Is ka matlab

Teeno daur mein faida hua. Is liye system mazboot mana gaya. Ye qaida test se pehle likha gaya tha.

2000 ke baad faida chhota hai, taqreeban +0.20R har trade. Saal mein ~8 trades hain, to 1% risk par taqreeban +2% saal ka andaza hai. Woh +506% zyada tar 1970s ki tezi aur 50 saal ke compounding se aaya.

Tester mein sab se gehri girawat 27% thi. Lagatar kai haar aur saal bhar ka nuqsan is system mein aam hai.

2R par aadhi band? Nahi.

Tester ne dono naape. 2R par aadhi band: +274%, PF 1.59. Poori trade chalne di: +506%, PF 1.68. Girawat sirf 27% se 24.5% hui. Is liye panel mein switch BAND hai.

Demo aur live

Pehle demo par haath saaf karein

  • Demo par maqsad paisa nahi, tareeqa hai. Saal mein 8 trades se kuch "sabit" nahi hota. Sabit karne ka kaam 459 trades wala Tester kar chuka hai.
  • Har demo trade ke baad: MT5 ka nateeja R mein nikalein (faida ÷ shuru ke SL ka paisa) aur panel ki label se milayein. 0.2R se zyada farq ho to wajah dhoondein: der se entry, SL na sarkaya, ya swap.

  • Live par bhi risk 1% se zyada nahi. Settings ghuma kar purane number achhe karna mana hai.
Aam ghaltiyan

Ye na karein

  • SL peeche le jana "thora aur dekh lete hain" ke liye. SL sirf aage jata hai.
  • 2R ya kisi bhi faide par khud band karna. Bari jeet hi saari kamai hai.
  • Candle band hone se pehle entry. Chalti candle naali ke upar ja kar wapas aa sakti hai.
  • 1H ya 15m par lagana. Ye sirf 1D par naapa gaya hai.
  • Signal chhor dena kyunke pichli 3 haar gayin. Agli bari jeet wahi ho sakti hai.
  • Tide ki trade aur tokri ki lots ek account mein. Desk ke tools dono ko mila dete hain.
