JAS Trade Roznamcha

Hamare 5 EA ki har trade, 2012 se aaj tak, hamare apne data (Dukascopy) par. Kisi trade par click karein, us ka chart, entry, SL aur band qeemat neeche dikhegi. Ek waqt mein ek trade, is liye chart saaf rahega.

Hisaab

Exp = har trade ka ausat faida, R mein (1R = SL ka nuqsan), kharcha aur swap nikal kar. Exp-2SE > 0 (hara) = faida pakka, qismat nahi. PnL % = $100k account par us EA ke risk se kul faida. DD % = sab se bari girawat. SELL (S1, S2) test mein FAIL (#73), sirf dekhne ke liye.

Trade

* = trade abhi khuli hai (data 30 Oct tak), R abhi tak ka. Chart 4H candles ka hai: trade se 30 candle pehle aur 12 baad. Laal patti = entry se SL tak (khatra). Hari/laal doosri patti = entry se band qeemat tak (asal nateeja).
